# Fix: the benchmark's findings matcher NITs carried in #190

## In short

Issue #190 collected fifteen `NIT` findings, plus one tidy item, from the reviews of #188 and #193
on the benchmark under `tests/bench/`. None of them affects a shipped skill. Three were real
misreadings by the findings matcher:

- A findings table headed `| Item | Details |` was folded into a single finding.
- `### 4.1 Open questions` under a findings heading was read as a finding.
- In a chain, a step whose session left no closing message was measured against the last step's
  message.

The rest were comments in the wrong place, wording that had gone stale, and checks that had only
ever been run once by hand.

This change fixes fourteen of them. It also shares the leading-number prefix across four patterns,
and it leaves N14 as it is, since the Tech Lead decided to keep it. The run found nothing on the
saved reports that changes: `findingsIn` gives identical output on all 160 of them. Two review
rounds found nothing blocking. Lam Ngoc Khuong, as Tech Lead, answered the two questions round 1
raised, section 6b: `named-failure` stays as written, and `tests/verify.sh` now runs
`check-chain.mjs`. That answer, and the two items section 9 had left for later, which the Tech Lead
asked to finish here rather than file, are changes made after the review, listed in "After the last
round". Lam Ngoc Khuong approves it.

## After the last round

Round 2 fixed nothing, so there was nothing for a closing read.

After the review, on the Tech Lead's answer of 2026-11-14, `tests/verify.sh` gained one check,
`OK bench chain machinery`, which runs `node tests/bench/check-chain.mjs`, and the lines that list
the free bench checks moved with it: two in `CLAUDE.md` under "Common verification commands", and
the `tests/bench/` and `tests/verify.sh` rows of both codebase summaries.

Then, on the Tech Lead's word of the same day, the two items section 9 had left for later were done
here rather than filed as issues:

- The per-step key warning of `--recheck` moved out of `run.mjs` into `changedKeys(was, now)` in
  `checks.mjs`, which returns the findings checks whose key hash changed, by step. `run.mjs` prints
  the same line as before from it, and `check-chain.mjs` holds it with three checks.
- `lastTextOf` gives a chain kept before each step had its own last message the run's last message
  for the last step that started, and none for the other steps. Before this, every step of such a
  run read nothing under `--recheck`, the last one included. `check-chain.mjs` holds it with four
  checks, the first of them red against the previous `lastTextOf`.

No review round read any of these lines. The first part adds a check and its description; the second
moves one block without changing what it prints, and narrows one fallback to records written before
this change.

## 1. Symptom as captured

The issue body and its comment, verbatim in their substance (the full text is on
https://github.com/lamngockhuong/aiteamkit/issues/190):

- N1: in `tests/bench/lib/checks.mjs`, `findingsCheck`'s comment heads `measureTexts`; in `lib/seed.mjs` the injection-check comment sits above the findings-key refusal.
- N2: when a turn's only content was a closing question, `detail` says "no report and no closing message".
- N3: the `why` of `worded-citations.md` names `src/server.js (L19)`; the case cites `src/orders.js (L19)`.
- N4: the `tests/verify.sh` row of both codebase summaries does not name the bench findings matcher check.
- N5: `named-failure` in `tests/bench/rubrics/review.md` says nothing is counted when no key defect is cited, while the judge prompt says an item with no evidence is false. `PLAUSIBLE`.
- N6: per-step findings groups, the listing of missed defects, the branch filter, the key-branch refusal, the per-step key warning and the two-step findings check are held only by one-off `node -e` runs.
- N7: a comment in `checks.mjs` and the `tests/bench/` row of both codebase summaries still say a turn ending in a question is left out whole.
- N8: `FIELD_TABLE` accepts `item` and `VALUE_HEADER` accepts `details`, so a findings table headed `| Item | Details |` folds into one finding. `PLAUSIBLE`.
- N9: an earlier chain step whose turns all have an empty `result` falls back to `run.lastText`, the last step's message (the judge does the same). `PLAUSIBLE`.
- N10: the `why` of `title-words.md` still says section words close no heading nested in a findings section.
- N11: two comments and one matcher case's `why` still say "right after", which the set-aside rule no longer means.

- N13: a dotted sub-number such as `4.1 Open questions` is not recognised as a section heading.
- N14: `Finding ruled out`, `Findings: ruled out` and `Findings we dropped` still open findings. Kept as it is by Lam Ngoc Khuong on 2026-11-08.
- N15: one comment line over the file's usual length.
- Tidy: the leading-number prefix `(?:\d+[.)]?\s*)?` is written inline in four regexes; one shared constant keeps them from drifting.

Environment: `main` at `2e8a424`, Node v24.14.1, Linux.

## 2. Root cause

There is one block per item. Each item was proved on its first hypothesis.

- **N1.** Code inserted by #188 separated two comments from the code they describe: `tests/bench/lib/checks.mjs:721-724` before the fix, and `tests/bench/lib/seed.mjs:108-110`.
- **N2.** `findingsCheck` builds its `detail` from `read.length` alone (`checks.mjs:752` before the fix). A turn that `withoutQuestion` reduced to nothing is filtered out by `measureTexts`, so it reads exactly like a run with no message at all.
- **N3, N10, N11.** Each `why` in `tests/bench/matcher-cases/expected.json` was written before a later fix changed the rule it describes. The same holds for the block comment at `checks.mjs:546-547`.
- **N4, N7.** The rows in `docs/codebase-summary.md:283-284` and `docs/vi/codebase-summary.md:284-285` were not updated when #188 added the matcher check and narrowed what is left out of a turn. The comment at `checks.mjs:721-723` was not updated either.
- **N5.** `tests/bench/rubrics/review.md:15` excludes uncited defects but does not say what happens when there are none. The judge prompt (`tests/bench/lib/judge.mjs:57-58`) makes an item with no evidence false.
- **N6.** The cases were run once as `node -e` during #188 and were never put in a file.
- **N8.** `FIELD_TABLE` (`checks.mjs:535`) lists `item`. A two-column header `Item | Details` therefore sets `fieldTable`, and every row after it continues the block above as a field.
- **N9.** `run.mjs:176` overwrites `record.lastText` on every turn, so after a chain it holds the last step's message. `findingsCheck` (`checks.mjs:748`) and `material` (`judge.mjs:52`) fall back to it for any step.
- **N12.** No case pinned the behaviour that was accepted.
- **N13.** The prefix `(?:\d+[.)]?\s*)?` accepts no `.1`, so `4.1 Open questions` misses `SECTION_ALONE` and `SECTION_OPENS`. It then reaches `TITLED` and is read.
- **N15.** `checks.mjs:557` was about 155 characters long.

## 4. Why it surfaced now

The NITs were recorded by the review loops of #188 (`5159d6e`) and #193 (`3efd19e`). The loop does
not fix a `NIT`, so they were carried into the issue. N8, N9 and N13 have been present since the

No saved report used either of the two shapes, so no run was ever misread by them.

## 5. The change

Each item's change is the smallest that removes its cause:

- **N1.** Both comments are moved back above their code. The `findingsCheck` comment also takes
  the N7 wording.
- **N2.** `detail` now says "every closing message was left out as a question to a person" when
  some turn text existed but none survived `withoutQuestion`.
- **N3, N10, N11.** The three `why` texts in `expected.json` are corrected, along with the block
  comment above `OPENS_FINDINGS`, which now points at `FINDINGS_SET_ASIDE`.
- **N4, N7.** Both codebase summary rows are corrected, English and Vietnamese.
- **N5.** `named-failure` says a run whose findings cite no defect of the key holds the item.
- **N6.** `check-chain.mjs` gains eight checks:
  - the seed's branch filter, and its refusal of a branch `history.json` does not have;
  - the refusal of a findings key that belongs to another branch;
  - a findings check on each of two steps, each reading its own file and session;
  - the fallback to the step's own last message, in the findings check and in the judge's
    material;
  - the report's per-step findings groups, and its listing of the defects each step missed.

  `start()` and `rows()` take a scenario, defaulting to the chain. After the review, seven more:
  four on `lastTextOf` for old, new and single-step records, and three on `changedKeys`, the
  per-step key warning of `--recheck`, now outside `run.mjs`.
- **N8.** `item` is dropped from `FIELD_TABLE`.
- **N9.** `run.mjs` keeps `lastText` per step, which is stripped from `results.json` as the run's
  own already was. A new `lastTextOf(run, step)` in `checks.mjs` is what the findings check and
  `judge.mjs` fall back to.
- **N12.** A new case, `numbered-title-section-name.md`.
- **N13, the tidy item and N15.** The four patterns now share one `NUMBER` prefix, which also
  accepts a dotted number. Case: `dotted-section-number.md`. The rewrapped comment fixes N15.
- **N8's case.** `item-details-table.md`.

N14 is unchanged, as decided.

After the review, `tests/verify.sh` runs `check-chain.mjs` as `OK bench chain machinery`, on the
Tech Lead's answer in section 6b, and `CLAUDE.md` and both codebase summaries name it among the
free bench checks.

Tidy: `/simplify` ran with four agents over the `tests/bench/` diff. It applied two changes inside
the changed lines: one local for `lastTextOf` in `material`, and `refusal()` in `check-chain.mjs`
reusing `start()`. It skipped three candidates:

- the double `lastText` write in `run.mjs`, since `record.lastText` still feeds the classifier;
- building the `detail` string only in its branch, which saves nothing worth the change;
- putting `TITLED` on the shared prefix: `\d+\b` already stops at the dot, so no verdict changes.

Reference documents: the fix changes no public contract. The benchmark has no reference document;
the codebase summary rows are the only docs it moves, and they move here.

## 8. Blast radius

- `findingsIn` and the regex constants: called by `measureTexts` (`checks.mjs`), which
  `findingsCheck` and `check-matcher.mjs` call. Exercised by every matcher case and by the
  160-report comparison.
- `FIELD_TABLE`: used only in `findingsIn`. Exercised.
- `lastTextOf`: called by `findingsCheck` and `judge.mjs` `material`. Exercised by `check-chain.mjs`
  for a step, for an old chain record, and for a single run.
- `changedKeys`: called by `recheck` in `run.mjs`. Exercised by `check-chain.mjs`; the stderr line
  around it is read, not run, since `--recheck` calls the paid judge.
- `findingsCheck` `detail`: read by `report.mjs` in the missed listing. The new message itself is
  not exercised by any case (round 1 N1).
- `run.mjs` per-step `lastText` and its strip from the kept record: `--dry-run` loads the module;
  not exercised past the first turn.
- `seed.mjs`: a comment move only.
- `rubrics/review.md`: read by the judge, not exercised (paid).
